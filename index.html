WELCOME TO DUET

Dhaka University of Engineering & Technology (DUET) is one of the most prestigious public engineering universities in Bangladesh, known for shaping highly skilled engineers and innovators. It offers a strong academic environment where students gain both quality education and practical research opportunities. With its modern campus, disciplined culture, and dedicated faculty, DUET inspires students to think creatively, solve real-world engineering problems, and contribute to the technological development of the nation.

ABOUT DUET

DUET was established in 1963 as an engineering institute in Bangladesh. Later, it was upgraded to a full public engineering university. The university is well known for its quality education and research activities. DUET has a beautiful and peaceful campus environment for students. Students enjoy various cultural, technical and sports activities on campus. The teachers are experienced, friendly and supportive to the students. Modern laboratories and classrooms help students gain practical knowledge. DUET encourages innovation, creativity and technological development. The university produces many skilled and responsible engineers every year.
DUET plays an important role in the development of Bangladesh.
